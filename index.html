SIH Problem Statement 26015 · Ministry of Rural Development

From Field Evidence to Watershed Decisions

JALNETRA brings together field photos, Sentinel-2 satellite imagery, and GIS layers to help officers analyse land cover, vegetation, water spread, and change — for evidence-based watershed planning.

🛰
Satellite Intelligence
NDVI · NDWI · Land Cover
Continuous multispectral data from Sentinel-2 surface reflectance imagery.
📍
Geo-coded Evidence
GPS · Field Photos · Observations
EXIF geotag parsing from field photos — no fabricated coordinate fallbacks.
🔄
Change Analysis
12-Month Trends · Before/After
Pixel-level tracking of surface water and vegetation regeneration over time.
🎯
Decision Support
Hotspots · Interventions · Reports
Automated summaries for administrative action with cross-sensor validation.
⚡ How It Works
Sentinel-2 · 10m Resolution
STEP 1
Data Ingestion
Sentinel-2 L2A + Field Photos + GIS Layers
Raw spectral bands (B02, B03, B04, B08) paired with on-ground asset geotags.
STEP 2
Spatial Analysis
NDVI + NDWI + Land Cover + Change Detection
Pixel-level semantic masks and cloud-filtered 12-month evolution computed automatically.
STEP 3
Decision Output
Hotspots + Intervention Plan + Officer PDF
Generates certified administrative reports with cross-sensor evidence validation.
